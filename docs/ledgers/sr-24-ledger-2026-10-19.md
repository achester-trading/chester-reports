## Caveats

- The survey's CPI was not seasonally adjusted before December 2004; the control window crosses that change, and CPIAUCNS is used throughout.
- BP is the documentation's last value known when the questionnaire was sent; in early surveys the panelists may have known a slightly different month, which the documentation flags for the pre-2004 history of every variable except CPI.
- A median of panelists, not a market price: the survey records what economists said, twice a year.

The episodes are in `sr-24-ledger-2026-10-19-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
