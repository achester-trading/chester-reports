## Results

Current z (as of 2026-09): gold +2.44, S&P 500 +1.98, Treasuries +0.26, commodities +0.72.

## Caveats

- The box re-run reads the same public sources (the calibration rule), not the store.
- The 10-year real yield is ex post (GS10 less trailing CPI) throughout, so its z is not the TIPS yield's.
- Paired months overlap: 36 consecutive months share most of their forward window.
