## Caveats

- G.19 is heavily revised and its levels have breaks (benchmark revisions); the latest vintage smooths both, which is why the rule is on first prints.
- Rolling z-scores inflate after quiet stretches (the register's own note on 2023-25).
- Thresholds enter config only on a pass, with this ledger named beside them; on a fail or an insufficient n they stay where they are.

The episodes are in `sr-5-ledger-2026-10-18-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
