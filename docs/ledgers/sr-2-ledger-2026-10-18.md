## Caveats

- DEXJPUS is the noon New York rate, where the live tell reads Yahoo's JPY=X close; the two differ intraday, not in a 5-session move.
- The 2007-08 window is long (two unwinds, Aug 2007 and Oct 2008), so flagging it is easier than flagging the two-month windows.
- Thresholds enter config only on a pass, with this ledger named beside them; on a fail or an insufficient n they stay where they are.
