## Caveats

- French restates the whole HML history with each CRSP update; the vintage is printed, and a box edition may differ by it.
- HML is a long-short paper portfolio rebalanced annually; the ETF pairs are the tradeable expression, and their sample begins in 2000, so the late-1990s check is HML's alone.
- Thresholds enter config only on a pass, with this ledger named beside them; on a fail or an insufficient n they stay where they are.

The episodes are in `sr-1-ledger-2026-10-18-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
