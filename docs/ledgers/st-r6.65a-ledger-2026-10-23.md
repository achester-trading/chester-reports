## Caveats

- Current vintages: both models are re-estimated, so a historical share is not the one a reader saw that day. The live ledger (rates.speed_event_ledger) records each day's own read.
- The two models decompose the same yields: their agreeing is not two independent witnesses.
- The flag is computed on the full DGS10 history from 1962, so its window is full from 1990 on.

The episodes are in `st-r6.65a-ledger-2026-10-23-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
