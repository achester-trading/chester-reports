## Caveats

- The baskets are French's SIC-based industries, not LSEG's sectors; the chart's ~150 pp is a different construction, so the levels are not comparable, only the episodes' timing.
- The top decile is the full sample's, as the register's own list is; an expanding-window decile would admit fewer early episodes.
- Thresholds enter config only on a pass, with this ledger named beside them; on a fail or an insufficient n they stay where they are.

The episodes are in `sr-13-ledger-2026-10-18-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
