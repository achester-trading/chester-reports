## Caveats

- A labelled proxy: the close to the next open includes the after-hours and pre-market sessions whole, not the 16:00 to 21:30 window the note reads.
- The window is filled only as far back as Yahoo's earnings calendar reaches for each name (the per-name counts are above); prints before the five years are dropped.
- Two samples are never pooled: the evening note's own readings accrue from its first live edition.
