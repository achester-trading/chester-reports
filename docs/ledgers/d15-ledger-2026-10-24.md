## Caveats

- Current vintages: both models are re-estimated, so a historical share is not the one a reader saw that day. The live ledger (rates.oneday_event_ledger) records each day's own read.
- The two models decompose the same yields: their agreeing is not two independent witnesses.
- KW publishes THREEFYTP10 on its own calendar, so a one-day split is read only where both the day and the day before are stored (within 4 days); a one-day premium change is small and noisy against the models' daily fit.

The episodes are in `d15-ledger-2026-10-24-episodes.csv`, beside this file.
