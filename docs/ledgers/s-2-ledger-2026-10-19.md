## Sources and vintages

## Results

- Daily total flows: 710 sessions, 2024-01-11 to 2026-10-16; 5-day sums ranked point-in-time from the 20th sum (687 ranked days).
- Extreme days: 37 at or above the 95th, 68 at or below the 5th; 22 non-overlapping episodes, 22 with a completed 20-day window.
- 22 completed episodes (9 high, 13 low); 20-day mean after high +4.5%, after low -2.4%; asymmetry +6.8%; Welch t +1.14.
- After high episodes, 5 days: mean -1.3%, median -0.6%, mean max drawdown -3.3% (n=9).
- After high episodes, 20 days: mean +4.5%, median -1.3%, mean max drawdown -7.6% (n=9).
- After low episodes, 5 days: mean +1.1%, median -0.0%, mean max drawdown -3.8% (n=13).
- After low episodes, 20 days: mean -2.4%, median -2.8%, mean max drawdown -11.6% (n=13).
