# OS-9 against the vendor's large-activity table: the December comparison, pre-registered (Batch 11 item 58)

*Written on 19 Oct 2026 by session A on branch `a48-batch11`, before any vendor value exists to compare with. **Never edited.** A change is a new specification in a new dated file. The spec is signal-triage Batch 11 (`docs/change-order-signal-triage-batch-11-2026-10-19.md`), item 58.4 and decision D2, ruled 19 Oct 2026, 13:05 ET. It is built with the vendor-month writer ruled on 18 Oct 2026 and runs only during that month.*

## What it compares

**For each session of the vendor month**, OS-9's eight printed rows ("Largest OI builds, side unknown", for the 17 captured names) are set beside the vendor's large-activity table for the same names and the same session.

The vendor's table is stored only for this comparison (D2): in its own keys, under the vendor's source id, read by this ledger and by nothing else. **Nothing of the vendor's prints in a report, and no committed edition of this ledger prints a vendor level, contract or figure.** Each edition prints agreement statistics only.

## The statistics, as registered

1. **The overlap of contracts.** A contract is its name, expiry, strike and type. Per session, the share of OS-9's printed contracts that appear in the vendor's table, and the share of the vendor's contracts (for the same names) that appear in OS-9's eight rows. The month's figure is the pooled share, with its n (contracts) and the number of sessions.
2. **How often a known side would have changed the read.** For each OS-9 contract that the vendor's table also carries with a side (bought or sold, as the vendor classifies it), whether the side disagrees with what a reader of OS-9's row would most plausibly assume. That assumption is fixed here: **an opening build is read as a purchase**. This is the default a side-unknown table invites, and the reason OS-9 says "side unknown". The figure is the share of such contracts where the vendor's side is "sold", with its n.
3. **The size check.** Among the overlapping contracts, the rank correlation (Spearman) between OS-9's OI change and the vendor's reported size, with its n.

## The verdict, pre-registered

The comparison is **descriptive**: it grants nothing and changes nothing in OS-9.

The verdict word records only whether the month ran:
- **COMPLETE:** 15 or more vendor-month sessions with both tables stored.
- **INSUFFICIENT:** fewer than 15.

The three statistics print beside the verdict. **Any change to OS-9** (a side column, a filter, a different floor) **is a later ruling on this evidence**, never this ledger's.
