Closed by hand 1 Oct 10:50:07 ET at market, **BOT 100 at 13,951.31 MXN** (exec `00025b49.6abf0521.01.01`): realised **−163,416.03 MXN = −8,951.36 USD** at USDMXN 18.2560 (the `MXN=X` 14:50 UTC one-minute bar's open; the store holds no FX series). **The loss came from the listing and the currency, not from SPY:** the MXN price rose 13.27%, which decomposes as USDMXN +5.9–6.5%, the MXN listing's basis to SPY's USD parity +5.1–6.9% (entered 4.5–6.1% below parity, closed 0.4% above), and SPY's own USD move +0.1–1.2% — the ranges bracket the unknown entry between the 16 and 17 Sep closes. - **INC-7 2026-10-01** — *closed positions persisted in Portfolio Truth from 24 Sep to 1 Oct.* The IBKR sync wrote rows only for the holdings a sync listed, so a holding that left the book kept its last nonzero row as its newest. **SPY@ARCA.USD +100**, sold 24 Sep 16:16:52 UTC, still read +100 from its last row (24 Sep 16:01 UTC) for a week; **SPY@MEXI.MXN −100**, bought back 1 Oct 14:50 UTC, still read −100 after the 21:31 UTC sync showed gross position value 0.0 and maintenance margin 542.84. **Consequence:** the register and the reports read a stale open position — the close's portfolio table listed both SPY legs with stale P&L beside a flat account, and `b5fa4365` stayed `active` for a week after its exit. **Fix** in the commit that records this entry: each sync's position list is the whole book at its `observed_at` — a holding open in the store and absent from the sync is written as a zero position (quantity, market value, unrealized P&L) at that sync's instant, refused when the sync lists nothing while gross position value is nonzero (a failed read is not an exit) — and the close's positions table shows only nonzero rows from the latest sync, which also hides the pre-fix stale rows. The first sync after the deploy writes both SPY legs closed. Gate: `validate_ibkr_portfolio` group G.
- **INC-8 2026-10-02** — *the currency guard checked two transitions, and its fallback invented a currency.* The INC-6 guard (`fd52349`, 1 Oct) compared a listing's currency with the decision's expression currency only when a row was written as a draft or became active; a close or any other supersede went unchecked. Separately, `tools/decide.py set-status` carried the expression currency forward as `old or "USD"`, and the register stored an empty one as `"USD"` too — so every row written before the guard, whose currency was empty, came forward as a dollar expression. The structured-exit back-fill of 2 Oct superseded the closed peso chain (`d65f1da1`, currency empty) with **`d673b498`, a `SPY@MEXI.MXN` close recorded with `expression_currency = USD`**. The guard did not fire, because a close was not one of its two transitions. The exit itself (price, time, currency MXN, fx) is correct, and `register/pnl.py` reads the exit fields rather than the expression currency, so the chain's realised P&L was never affected. **Lesson: a fallback that invents a value is worse than an empty field** — empty says "not recorded"; USD said something false that the guard then trusted. **Fix** (6d branch): an empty expression currency carries forward as empty, or as the listing's own suffix where it has one (`@….MXN` → MXN), and is stored as NULL, never as USD; one rule (`register.store.currency_mismatch`) judges every write, a close included, and `decide.py set-status` applies it before a dry run returns; `set-status --expression-currency` records a correction as a new row. Closing a wrong-listing position is not trapped, because its suffix-derived currency matches its listing. Gate: `validate_register` groups K and M — M shows, on a synthetic copy of the chain, that the corrective supersede leaves realised P&L identical. **The correction of `d673b498` to MXN is the operator's register write**, printed for the operator to run on the box after the deploy.
