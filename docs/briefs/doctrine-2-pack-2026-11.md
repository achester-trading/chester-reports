# The Doctrine #2 preparation pack (7–8 November 2026)

**Reading time.**
- **Decision list:** about 11 minutes for the decision list (D2-1 to D2-72). It can be taken in one read.
- **Supporting text:** about 21 minutes for Parts 1–7, the evidence behind the decisions.
- **Whole pack:** about 68 minutes including the annexes. The annexes are read only for an item the sitting pulls.
- **Basis:** 230 words a minute, 15,603 words in all, inside the two-hour sitting.

**What this is.** This is the pack for the second Doctrine sitting, refreshed with October's rulings (A67, 24 Oct 2026). It replaces the agenda of the 20 Oct edition (a52-doctrine-2-pack) and keeps every item of that edition that still applies (Part 8, with its full text in Annexes A and B). It gathers in one place everything October left "proposed", "session-set" or "to Doctrine #2".

**How to take it.** The sitting's shape follows the standing ruling of 24 Oct: "present decision sets as one list with recommendations; Ari accepts in one read unless he disagrees".
- Each item is a numbered decision, **D2-n**. Each states the value as set, the alternative, and a one-line recommendation.
- Parts 1 to 8 give the evidence behind each.
- A decision marked *block* is meant to be taken with the others in its block.

**Nothing here is ruled.** Every recommendation is a **rule-31 draft (for the operator's accept-all)**: a session's judgment draft that grants nothing until the operator rules on it. The Doctrine is not edited, and neither is any config.

**Sources.** Every quoted value names its notes file, by path under `docs/briefs/` unless another path is given.

A book positioned for that distribution that "changes stance rarely" beats one that is static or twitchy. Rebalancing to the stance inside the Macro dial's band at the monthly session harvests the dial's read at a cadence slower than its noise. Price, Time, and Edge (ch. 15) adds that calendar rebalancing is contrarian ("the rule says sell the winner and buy the loser") and partly anticipated by others. Options as Expression (15.7) sets the instrument: in Transition or Stressed, "the Doctrine requires a convexity-managed form".
- **Base rate:** not yet. It would be supplied by Book A's own ledger against the static 60/40 over four quarters (T8-12).
- **Claimed edge:** not yet. No ledger tests the rebalance. The Book A rules it rests on are candidates (Base Rates, Appendix C): A-1 rests on the rates-driver cell (FAIL, 5 episodes); A-3 on sr-12-ledger-2026-10-18.md (INSUFFICIENT, n 3); A-4 on st-r5.30b-ledger-2026-10-17.md (NO GATE, n 266).
- **target_n:** 40, read at the four-quarter review under S-1's draft.
- **Evidence status:** Untested.

##### `a_tb_override`: Top & Bottom override

- **Mechanism:** "Between monthly sessions the book does nothing unless the Top & Bottom report fires an override, in which case the stance moves to the override band at the next session or, for a bottom signal, immediately" (4.1). The claim is that the extremes the report names are rarer and more informative than the dial's monthly read, and that the framework "is stronger at bottoms than at tops" (5.3). Positioning & Flows (7.5) names a washed-out reading as "the condition under which the T&B bottom signal is most trusted". Options as Expression (16.5) sets the expression: "Move Book A to the top of its band with futures or the index, not options."
- **Base rate:** not yet. It would be supplied by the T&B harness, which is not built (T8-6). `baserate.sentiment_turning_points` bears on turns but is not this setup's.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet. false-bottom-audit-2026.md is FAIL, n 16 (S&P 2026-10-16): only breadth discriminates, and A-2 needs two.
- **target_n:** 40, read at the four-quarter review.
- **Evidence status:** Failed (its re-entry confirmation, SR-8); the override itself is Untested.

#### Book B

##### `b_breakout`: breakout from a defined base

- **Mechanism:** markets "trend further than fundamentals alone would imply". The persistence "is strongest after a pivotal point has been decisively cleared", and "the retail crowd's habit of fading strength and buying weakness creates the counterparty" (4.2). Price, Time, and Edge (8.3) places the edge in "the transfer of inventory" a base broken on expanded volume represents, not in its geometry. The Gamma card conditions it: in Positive gamma "Breakouts distrusted — wait for the retest"; in Negative, "Breakouts trusted" (5.2). The Dealer's Hand (5.1) gives the reason: in positive gamma "the breakout gets sold by dealers before it can run".
- **Base rate:** not yet. It would be supplied by the Book B scanner's graded candidates, each a ledger forecast. `baserate.streaks_and_gaps` (P(up | up) 54.2% against 52.4%) and `baserate.all_time_highs` bear on persistence in general.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing (Table 7b: "the kill rule reads at 40 graded").

##### `b_reclaim`: reclaim of a lost level with volume

- **Mechanism:** the same 4.2 claim, at a level the market lost and takes back. Price, Time, and Edge (8.3): "a reclaimed level that holds on a retest". Technical Indicators (ch. 5) rates volume confirmation "mild evidence", so the reclaim is read on its retest, not its volume alone.
- **Base rate:** not yet, as `b_breakout`.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing.

##### `b_failed_breakdown`: failed breakdown

- **Mechanism:** a break below an obvious level that closes back inside it marks trapped sellers. Technical Indicators (ch. 6) calls a level "*swept* (pierced intraday, closed back inside — the stop-run signature, and a mild contrarian flag". Price, Time, and Edge (8.3) lists "a failed breakdown that reverses" among the forms that represent an inventory transfer. The Daily Cascade paper (DC 5.7.2) conditions it on leadership: "A breakout led by staples and utilities is a fade candidate, not a chase."
- **Base rate:** not yet, as `b_breakout`.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing.

##### `b_style_regime_pair`: value against growth after a growth extreme breaks

- **Mechanism:** from SR-1, not the Doctrine. When the growth/value ratio stretches more than two standard deviations above its ten-year trend and then breaks (the ratio below its 40-week average, or more than 8% off its 52-week high), the unwind is traded as a pair: long IVE / short IVW, or IWD / IWF, with a hard stop on a new ratio high. The Doctrine's nearest text is 4.2's short side, where "a bearish view there is expressed as a pair". Base Rates (Appendix D, SR-1) records the ledger's result.
- **Base rate:** cite sr-1-ledger-2026-10-18.md, the unconditional value-minus-growth over the same windows (the ledger's own comparison).
- **Claimed edge:** "+4.2% at 6 months and +17.9% at 12 across 6 non-overlapping episodes" after 0.50% costs on HML (sr-1-ledger-2026-10-18.md, PASS, n 6, HML 2026-08). The ETF pairs read the other way, under their floor (ETFs 2026-10-16). The edge is claimed on HML only, at the floor, subject to R-1.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Passed (the gate, at n 6); the setup itself is Untested.

##### `b_consumer_rs_tell`: consumer relative-strength tell

- **Mechanism:** from SR-5. The tape pays for consumer growth (XLY against XLP, equal-weight discretionary against SPY, the homebuilders) before the credit data confirms it, so the relative strength turning is a narrow flag for Book B. Base Rates (Appendix D, SR-5) renders the tell only on a PASS. The Daily Cascade paper (DC 5.7.2) makes cyclical leadership a condition for trusting a breakout.
- **Base rate:** not yet. sr-5-ledger-2026-10-18.md would supply it once the box holds first prints.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet. sr-5-ledger-2026-10-18.md is INSUFFICIENT, n 19 (G.19 2026-08): the rule is on first prints, which the laptop edition lacks; on latest vintages its 10-year window reads FAIL.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Insufficient.

#### Book C

##### `c_pin_fade`: fade toward a gamma-implied pin

- **Mechanism:** "the mechanical hedging of options dealers creates predictable intraday and multi-day behavior — mean reversion and pinning when dealers are long gamma" (4.3). In positive gamma, "Extremes get faded not because anyone thinks the extreme is wrong but because someone is *forced* to fade it" (Dealer's Hand, 5.1). Spot "tends to return to" the peak-gamma strike, "particularly into the close" (Dealer's Hand, 7.3), and the pull is strongest into a monthly expiry (Dealer's Hand, 10.2). The Daily Cascade paper (DC 2.7.2) calls the fade toward it "the highest-probability intraday setup".
- **Base rate:** not yet. The dealer scorecard's H1 (dampened) and H4 (pinned) define it per session (dealer-scorecard-preregistration-2026-10.md), and a50's stored captures now date it.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet. The scorecard is accruing: nothing prints below 20 scored sessions, and "no rate is a decision" below 120 eligible.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing.

##### `c_negative_gamma_continuation`: continuation through a level, Negative gamma

- **Mechanism:** below the flip, dealers are short gamma, and hedging amplifies a move through a level rather than absorbing it: "Momentum is *created* by the hedging, not merely permitted" (Dealer's Hand, 5.2). A flip break calls for "a **change of playbook**" (Dealer's Hand, 7.5), not a trade. After confirmation, the continuation is followed. The Doctrine names this regime as the one in which "the event-reaction and continuation setups have their cleanest edge" (5.5). Gate C admits it only "after fifty decisions and a positive thirteen-week expectancy, or after IBKR Gate 1 passes and brackets are server-side, whichever is later" (9.5).
- **Base rate:** not yet. The scorecard's H2 (amplified) and H5 (flip crossing) define it.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing.

##### `c_event_reaction`: event reaction

- **Mechanism:** "the price reaction to scheduled and unscheduled information relative to what the positioning implied *should* have happened contains information that the headline does not" (4.3). Doctrine Rule 9: "Price reaction outranks the headline." Earnings (ch. 9): "**The tradeable object is the difference between the reaction and the reaction that positioning implied**". Base Rates (ch. 6): "**A beat is not a surprise**".
- **Base rate:** not yet. The Lab's event base rates (alpha.db's `event_base_rates`) carry the drift by event and bucket, with no registry id.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet. sn-1-ledger-2026-10-19.md (FAIL, n 159) and sn-2-ledger-2026-10-19.md (FAIL, n 193; "continuation 107/193, Wilson 95% 48-62% includes 50%") show no unconditional continuation or fade edge. Neither tests the setup's condition, a reaction that contradicts the positioning-implied expectation.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Failed (its unconditional neighbours, SN-1 and SN-2); the conditional setup is Untested.

#### Book D

##### `d_forced_flow`: forced flow

- **Mechanism:** "forced flows — index inclusion, the unlock and lock-up mechanics of a mega-IPO, closing-auction imbalances, expiry pins — create predictable pressure that a small book can trade at sizes that do not matter to anyone else" (4.4). Forced sellers "supply a counterparty whose behavior is predictable in direction and whose reservation price does not exist" (Price, Time, and Edge, ch. 15). Positioning & Flows (4.5) reads the absorption path for the post-event drift, and (2.13) calls the closing auction "the closest thing the system has to a direct reading of forced flow".
- **Base rate:** not yet. Positioning & Flows (9.4) says a year of the auction sampler's history is needed. The scorecard's H4 (pinned, monthly expiry) covers the expiry-pin leg.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing (its expiry leg, H4); the rest is Untested.

##### `d_prediction_market_divergence`: prediction-market divergence

- **Mechanism:** prediction markets, "read for divergence from the price of the corresponding financial instrument rather than for their own forecasts, occasionally reveal a mispricing worth a small, convex position" (4.4). The engine is "confined to discovery in every column … narrow signal rights pending a calibration archive" (5.3). The Daily Cascade paper (8.2) reads a divergence as "a flag that someone with money disagrees with the narrative".
- **Base rate:** not yet. The prediction-market archive would supply it (90 days, 20 shocks, a lead study, two reviews).
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Accruing.

##### `d_squeeze_exhaustion`: exhausted squeeze, short with a defined wing

- **Mechanism:** "occasionally a short with a defined wing when a squeeze has exhausted", with "a time stop" on every packet (4.4). Positioning & Flows (6.4) marks the squeeze by the change in borrow, not the level of short interest. Positioning & Flows (7.5) gives Book D's case as "a squeeze exhausting with borrow improving". Price, Time, and Edge (ch. 15 and ch. 20) sets the risk: "Nothing compels the counterparty to stop; several things compel the operator to", which is why the wing is defined.
- **Base rate:** not yet. It would be supplied by stored borrow and short-interest series graded against the squeeze's end, which are not built.
- **Claimed edge:** not yet.
- **target_n:** 40.
- **Evidence status:** Untested.

##### `d_tail_hedge`: tail-hedge budget

- **Mechanism:** a ring-fenced 0.4% a month tail-hedge budget (far out-of-the-money puts, long volatility, a gold increment), "sized so that the entire budget can expire worthless every month for a year at a cost of 5% of capital". "A tail thesis is a hedge budget, not a position" (4.4). Volatility (ch. 29): "Most tail hedges lose money. This is not a design flaw; it is the premium in Section 2 paid from the other side." Options as Expression (8.3) names the put backspread as the usual meaning of "a small long-volatility structure".
- **Base rate:** not yet. `baserate.vix_distribution` (Base Rates 1.2) bears on how often the hedge pays.
- **Claimed edge:** none claimed. The hedge's expectancy is negative by construction, and its case is convexity against the book.
- **target_n:** 40, but judged under S-1's draft on spend against payoff, not on the fixed kill.
- **Evidence status:** Untested.
