# The Doctrine #2 preparation pack (7–8 November 2026)

**What this is.** This pack gathers every open item for Doctrine #2 in one place, each with a draft recommendation, so that the session itself can be short. It was ordered on 20 Oct 2026 ("accept all", decision 13 of that morning's list) and written by session A on branch a52-doctrine-2-pack, as built on chain-3 (STITCH COMPLETE, `4f004a4`), a50-dealer-store and b48-scoreboard.

**Nothing here is ruled.** Every recommendation is labelled **rule-31 draft (for the operator's accept-all)**. Rule 31 is this project's convention for a session's judgment drafts: each grants nothing until the operator rules on it. The Doctrine is not edited, and neither is `config/setups.yaml`.

**How to read an item.** Each item gives the Doctrine's words with the section, what exists as built, the options, the evidence (each ledger with its verdict, n and as-of, as the ledger records them), and the draft. Ledgers are in `docs/ledgers/` and are cited by file name. "No ledger bears" means none was found.
