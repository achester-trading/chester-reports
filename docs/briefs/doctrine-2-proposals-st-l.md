# Proposals for the Doctrine monthly of 7-8 November 2026 (Tranche H)

**Rule-31 drafts (library-st-l, session B, 19 October 2026), for Ari's accept-all and the monthly's agenda.** The signal-triage order's Tranche H lists five by-hand items. Each is drafted here as a proposal: none is a rule, a register status or a right until the monthly rules on it. Each states the register's own words where it quotes them, and the evidence the ledgers on chain-2 give.

## 1. Tail-register edits (SR-3, China's external position)

The register's ruling, quoted: "CNY balance-of-payments crisis: score low — the state controls the capital account and official reserves alone cover external debt; any sharp devaluation is a choice, not a forced event. Domestic deflation / balance-sheet recession: keep fully live (the Koo case in the Debt Cycles brief). New scenario: deliberate devaluation to export deflation — Factor III/IV cross; hits commodities, EM FX and US inflation expectations together; tell defined in SR-4."

**Proposal.** Make the three edits to the tail register as worded. The new scenario's tell is SR-4's deliberate-devaluation tell, which prints "2 of 3 legs tracked" and never a verdict (ruling 6 of 18 October): its ledger is INSUFFICIENT, because spot CNH has no keyless history before 2024 (`docs/ledgers/sr-4-ledger-2026-10-18.md`). The scenario enters the register with that limitation stated.

## 2. The 5.25% note (SR-9)

The register's ruling rejects 5.25% as a line ("an era effect") and keeps "the level prior" as a by-hand note: "above ~5.25%, treat a positive correlation reading as persistent rather than transient."

**Proposal.** Keep the note as a heuristic and never a threshold, worded as the GTM-7 amendment's regime table already frames it (three eras of the 24-month stock-bond correlation, each beside its average inflation). The scatter regression that would test it (SR-9's validation gate) is in the backlog; until it runs, the note says "unvalidated".

## 3. The projection-rule rationale (SR-18)

The register adopts the buffer mechanism and rejects JPM's chart as a current reading (the §1.5 fixture: a projection circulated as a print).

**Proposal.** One sentence for the Doctrine's reading rules: "A projection is labelled as one where it prints, with who made it, when and under what assumption; a projection is never read as the series it projects." SR-18's own gate (days of cover in the bottom decile against a disruption) has no ledger: IEA days of cover was a by-hand line, which rule 30 does not create.

## 4. The false-bottom rule candidate (SR-8)

The register's candidate, quoted: "In a declared bear regime a rally is a mechanism, not a signal. Book A re-entry requires the bottom composite plus confirmation. Size on the assumption that a bear rally and a new bull are indistinguishable in real time."

**Evidence.** The false-bottom audit FAILS (`docs/ledgers/false-bottom-audit-2026.md`): across four bears it finds 12 false bottoms and 4 true ones, and one confirmation element, breadth (RSP against SPY), discriminates; the rule needs two. Its rights consequence: the specification stands as written, with its gaps, and the monthly decides; Book A rule A-2 changes nothing until then.

**Proposal.** Adopt the first and third sentences as a Doctrine principle, which the audit's count of false bottoms supports. Hold the second (re-entry on "the bottom composite plus confirmation") until a confirmation set discriminates: with one element, the audit gives it nothing to require.

## 5. The ETF record-low note (SR-22)

The register's note, quoted: "Drawdown of a constant-duration fund is yield arithmetic; a 'record low' in price is not a level that means anything. Candidate rule, not a rule."

**Evidence.** SR-22's absorption ledger FAILS for the contrarian read (`docs/ledgers/sr-22-ledger-2026-10-18.md`: median 3-month 10-year change −2.0 bp, a fall in 51.8% of 1,034 auctions, against the rule's 60%), so the stress flag stays display only, and ABS_STRESS prints its firing base rate beside it.

**Proposal.** Adopt the note as a reading rule: a price low in a constant-duration fund is read as a yield level, never as a level of its own.
