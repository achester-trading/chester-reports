Title: Turning Points, Bubbles, and Crises. Shelf: Markets. Numeral XXVI. Purpose: the market paper on how cycles end and begin - the anatomy of bubbles, the mechanics of crises, and the reading of tops and bottoms from inside - written for the operator who must act at them, with the four books defined where first used.
Parts. I What X established: its eight episodes and the near-misses, kept and re-read with what the harness (XXXI) has since learned: bottoms are catchable, most tops are not, and the 2007 top is structurally uncatchable. II Bubbles as an anatomy: the Minsky-Kindleberger sequence (displacement, boom, euphoria, profit-taking, panic) as mechanism, with the signposts the stack carries at each stage (valuation, concentration, issuance, leverage, the froth gauge, the Capitulation Count), and a hold-and-trade playbook: how Book A holds through, how Book B trades the stages, what Book C does at the edges; the BofA peak-concentration table (item 31) and the bear-rally structure (SR-8) as the routed items. III Banking crises as a mechanism: 2023's regional run, 2008, Continental Illinois, and the sovereign-bank loop - the common structure (duration or credit loss meeting a funding mismatch) and the tells the bank-stress panel (item 59) prints. IV Liquidity crises: the 2019 repo spike, March 2020, the 2022 gilt episode - the plumbing's failure modes, what the policy put did each time and how fast, read against the policy-put panel (item 124d) and the forward TGA path (item 121). V Emerging-market crises: the balance-of-payments template, the dollar-funding channel, the EM shock screen (item 10) and its AI-chain sub-panel (item 129). VI International tops: when the leading market tops before the US, and the AI chain's four markets trading as one position (2026 as the live case, dated). VII Reading a turn from inside: the Capitulation Count (item 97) beside the Top & Bottom composite, the opener problem from the tail-days study (item 124: five earlier openers, 5 Nov 2026 the first at -4% or worse), base rates not analogs (the first-month-drawdown row, item 130). VIII Tactical Applications by horizon, Wiring (which reports print which signposts), Environments. Appendix A Assumptions and Falsifiers. Appendix B dated: the 2026 episode as it stands at the as-of date, no forecast.
Boundaries. Debt Cycles, Regimes and Environments (XXVII) owns long-cycle tops and the debt-cycle lens: XXVI points to its Part III and does not re-narrate 1929, 1989, 2000 or 2007 as long-cycle tops; it reads them as turning points only. XXXI owns the T&B methodology. Cite both by name.
