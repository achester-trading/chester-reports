# 6e: the metric lenses

**This branch builds on aud-3a-catalogue and merges after next week's chain,
through a refresh.** Branch `6e-metric-lenses` is cut from
`origin/aud-3a-catalogue` at **3c85b21**, because the lenses read AUD-3a's split
units (`level_units_of`, `delta_unit_for`). It was planned for next week and pulled
forward.

Branch only: nothing merged, nothing deployed, nothing run on the box.

## What and why

The brief (`docs/briefs/metric-lenses-brief-2026-10-01.md`, ruled 1 Oct) starts
from what every pillar metric used to show: the same two columns, a five-year
level percentile and a 20-session change, whatever the data.
- A 20-session change on a monthly release is noise.
- A level percentile on a trending quantity says little.
- A five-year percentile reads a 10-year yield as "100th" while it sits far below
  its long history.

Each family is now measured its own way. The Doctrine monthly ratified all of it
on 4 Oct (minutes item 6, 15:05 ET, "ratify all five as stated").

## 1. The families

The vocabulary is the registry's existing `vocabularies.family`: price, rate,
spread, release, quantity, conditions, ratio. A registry series carries its family
in one of three places:
- the **`families:` map**, key → family, for individually registered metrics. Batch
  3 began it, `check_registry` holds it, and it now has 734 lines.
- a block's **`family`**, for a bulk block of one family (the closes, the OHLC, the
  volumes, the correlations).
- a mixed block's **`member_families`**: the seven FRED blocks member by member,
  and the closes' ^TNX and ^TYX as rates.

A series that is not a level gets no family and is named in a new
**`lens_excluded`** section with its reason. That covers flags, labels, dates,
corporate actions, tables and a log's columns.

`derived.resolve_registry` now resolves all of this. A metric's own `family` wins
over the map, and the map wins over a block's. `derived.family_of()` and
`long_window_of()` read it.

## 2. The forms: `derived.lens_forms()`

They live in `altdata/derived.py` only, beside `derived_forms` and
`delta_percentile`. The renderer only prints.

| Family | Forms |
|---|---|
| price | returns 1m / 3m / 12m / YTD; drawdown from the 52-week high with **correction** and **bear** flags; 20-day realized volatility (annualized) and its percentile among five years of 20-day vols; the percentile of the 12-month return over the full history (6c); the 1-month move's own percentile through `delta_percentile` |
| rate | bp changes 1m / 3m / 12m; percentiles five-year and full history |
| spread | percentiles five-year and 20-year; bp changes 1m / 3m; the **widening** flag per series |
| release | year over year (percent for a level, pp for a rate) and 3-month annualized (a 3-month pp change for a rate); the surprise against consensus where one is held; the **revision line** ("Aug revised 320.0 -> 320.5") when the vintage that brought the latest print changed a prior one; the unemployment rate adds a level percentile (6e) |
| quantity | year-over-year change; the 3-month change in its registry units; direction of travel |
| conditions | percentiles five-year and 20-year; z-score over five years; spike against the 1-year average |
| ratio | percentiles five-year and 20-year (valuation ratios full history); the 3-month trend |

**The rules, as built:**
- **Dual percentiles.** `pctile_5y` and `pctile_long`, each with `_window`, `_n` and
  a precomputed `_ordinal` (numeral_audit's one helper).
- **Long windows (6c):** spreads and conditions 20y; rates full; inflation full;
  labour and activity 20y; the 12-month-return percentile full; ratios 20y;
  quantities none. The registry's `long_windows` map overrides per series (TIPS,
  CAPE, forward P/E full).
- **The disagreement flag (6d)** fires when the two percentiles are 30 points
  apart.
- **The move's own percentile**, through `delta_percentile`.
- **Point-in-time:** every form reads the as-of join, and the revision line reads
  only vintages knowable at the cutoff. Both of its numbers are stored vintages;
  nothing is rewritten.
- **Units from the registry:** bp scale from `units`, the 3-month change in the
  registry's units word.
- **A form that cannot be computed** carries its reason in `reasons`. The table
  prints `n/a` and puts the reason in the table's footnote.
- **The surprise:** the events table holds earnings consensus but no consensus for
  a macro release. So the surprise prints "the events table holds no consensus for
  this series" unless a caller supplies one; the fixture does.
- **Direction of travel** is not in the brief, so it is session-set: rising when
  the 1-month and 3-month changes are both above zero, falling when both are below,
  turning otherwise. Needs-you 3.

## Not named by the paste, so chosen here

1. **Direction of travel's rule** (rising, falling, turning), above.
2. **Realized volatility** is annualized from 20 daily log returns, and its
   percentile is taken among five years of 20-day windows.
3. **A release's YoY** is in pp for a rate (the unemployment rate) and in percent
   for a level. Its 3-month form is annualized for a level and a plain pp change
   for a rate.
4. **The revision line** covers the three periods before the latest, and prints in
   the series' level form.
5. **The 295 boolean flags with a declared family** read as no lens, and their
   declaration is left alone.
6. **`until_the_lenses`** is read as the family default.
