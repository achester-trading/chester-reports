### SR-30 — EU sovereign stress (OAT–Bund)
- **Source:** Elliott Wave International chart, OAT–Bund 10y weekly close, posted 2 Oct 2026. Levels verified on the wires.
- **Claim:** A France-specific fiscal premium — the 2027 budget (€43bn austerity package, €340bn net medium/long issuance planned) was shown and the market widened anyway; the spread (147–149bp on 2 Oct, widest since 2012) is now wider than Italy's and Greece's; Bunds are not catching a safety bid (10y Bund ~3.6%, near the highest since 2009); EUR/CHF is transmitting it. The 2011–12 analog fails on two counts: this is the core, not the periphery, and the move is a premium on top of a hot-inflation eurozone rather than a flight to quality.
- **Measure:** OAT–Bund 10y and BTP–Bund 10y, weekly close; EUR/CHF as the transmission tell. Dual percentile: five-year plus a 20-year window.
- **Rights:** narrow flag in plumbing & rates; input to Factor IV and to the debt-cycle lens in Debt Cycles, Regimes & Environments. Two-tier threshold, both on the weekly close (ruled 6 Oct 2026): 150bp is the watch line (post-2012 territory); ~190bp is the crisis line (the Nov 2011 peak, exact level verified and set at filing) and is the tier that earns a sentence in the Monthly's "what doesn't fit". The parliamentary-fracture angle is routed to Internal Order (Factor VI); no new tail scenario.
- **Gate:** calibration ledger over 2010–12 and 2024–26 before any panel.

### SR-31 — Halving-cycle base rate
- **Source:** X post (Alex Mason), 8 Sep 2026: buy 500 days before the halving, sell 500 days after; annotated chart projecting a 2026 bottom, 2029 top, 2030 bottom.
- **Claim as logged:** three completed cycles is not a base rate. The 2024 cycle landed roughly on schedule (peak ~Oct 2025, ~530 days post-halving) but at ~1.9× the prior ATH versus 3.4× and 20× before — diminishing returns is the finding. ### SR-32 — Japan slow-money repatriation (new family `jp.slowmoney`)
- **Sources:** Reuters analysis, 25 Sep 2026 ("falling knife"); Normandie Research, 30 Sep 2026 ("The Bid Is Going Home"); Daiwa life-insurer plan summaries (Apr 2026).
- **Claim:** the fast-money carry trade is unwound (speculative yen positioning flipped to a $9.7bn net long, the largest since July 2025, in the first two weeks of September); the slow money — lifers (¥438.6tn of assets), pensions, unhedged households — has not started. With hedging costs near 3%, a 10y Treasury at ~4.8% yields ~2% in yen against a 3.10% JGB; the adjustment shows up as non-reinvestment, lower hedge ratios and rotation of new money home while the stock of holdings barely moves (RBC: still ~$50bn net foreign-bond buying over the twelve months to late summer; HSBC: Japanese banks sold ~$70bn of foreign bonds YTD). The marginal buyer of everyone's long end has left; the stock hasn't.
- **Sub-signal, the 4.5% line:** market convention that lifers become forced sellers above ~4.5% on the 30y JGB; 4.18% on 30 Sep after a record 4.22%. Mechanism is accounting, not sentiment: economic-value solvency improves with yields until unrealised losses on the bond and hedge books dominate; an unhedged dollar book makes a sharp yen rally the second blade. Analog: June 2003, 10y JGB 0.43% → >1.6% in about two months on correlated VaR selling. Wrinkle: foreigners put a record ¥9.3tn into Japanese superlongs in 2025 — price-sensitive money now funds Japan's own long end.
- **Measures:** (a) MoF weekly portfolio flows (the only near-real-time read on rotation vs selling); (b) the major lifers' semi-annual investment plans, April and October — domestic vs foreign, hedge ratios (April: lifers net sellers of foreign bonds six fiscal years running, only two of the top ten planning to add hedged foreign bonds, none unhedged); (c) GPIF allocation statements; (d) 20y and 40y JGB auction tails; (e) 30y JGB vs 4.5%, daily; (f) USD/JPY vs the year's range.
- **Rights:** narrow flags in plumbing & rates and positioning & flows. The 4.5% line goes live before the family is built (ruled 6 Oct 2026): 30y JGB daily, an approach line at 4.40% and the 4.5% flag, each printed with the one-line mechanism (above ~4.5% unrealised losses on lifers' bond and hedge books dominate the solvency benefit of higher yields). It is a convention the market has chosen, so it earns a flag before the family exists.
- **Cross-check (SR-32 ↔ SR-30):** the five-step chain — weak 20/40y JGB auctions with lengthening tails → lifers revising semi-annual plans → OAT–Bund widening on a day nothing happened in Paris → US 30y auction tails → yen appreciation past this year's range. Wired as a joint read: any two steps firing in the same fortnight is the flag.
- **Gate:** calibration over 2003, 2022–23 and Jan 2026 before any panel.

### SR-36 — Treasury basis-trade proxy (leveraged-fund futures short) — added r2
- **Source:** James E. Thorne (@DrJStrategy), X, 5 Oct 2026, with a CFTC Traders in Financial Futures chart (CBOT 10y note futures, leveraged funds, futures only): net short 2.04m contracts, ~36% of open interest, at the 29 Sep 2026 report; record 2.53m, ~45%, on 26 Aug 2025; 2008 peak ~12% of the market, 2022 selloff ~21%; the short rarely exceeded 1m contracts before 2023.
- **Claim as posted:** hedge funds dominate 10y futures; a short this large keeps yields high without fresh inflation prints, which is why soft data is "ignored"; a forced cover would be a squeeze of historic proportions.
- **Claim as logged:** the series is the cash-futures basis trade, not a directional short, and the directional reading is rejected on three counts. (1) Composition: the Fed's FEDS Note of 22 Jun 2026 estimates the basis trade at ~$830bn as of Sep 2025, about double its early-2020 peak and ~35% of hedge funds' long Treasury exposure, with swap-spread arbitrage a further ~$305bn; the OFR (19 Aug 2026) has hedge funds' cash Treasury holdings near $2tn against ~$1.4tn of short futures — long cash, short futures, repo-financed, duration-neutral by construction. No yield-level mechanism. (2) Falsifier in the chart itself: the 10y short has shrunk ~20% from the Aug 2025 record while the 10y yield made its highs in Sep–Oct 2026; Morgan Stanley (via Bloomberg, early Oct 2026) estimates leveraged basis notional fell to ~$900bn from ~$1.26tn at the start of 2026 as the futures–cash gap compressed — deleveraging, not covering, and yields rose anyway. (3) Sign of the unwind: a forced unwind buys back futures but sells repo-financed cash Treasuries; the cash leg dominates (March 2020, April 2025) — yields up, swap spreads wider, MOVE up, liquidity breaks, then a policy backstop. It is a dislocation-and-backstop sequence, not a bull squeeze. What survives is a fragility read: the size of the leveraged position sets how violent a vol or funding shock becomes.
- **Measures:** (a) leveraged-fund net position summed across TU, FV, TY, TN, US and WN as a share of combined open interest, contract-weighted and DV01-weighted (the 10y alone misses the 2y and 5y, where the basis is largest); (b) asset-manager net long as the mirror (basis exists because asset managers hold duration in futures); (c) the same by contract, for tenor concentration; (d) weekly change in the short against the change in open interest — short falling with OI falling reads as deleveraging, short falling with OI flat reads as covering; (e) dual percentiles, five-year plus full TFF history (2010–). Source: CFTC TFF, weekly (Fridays 15:30 ET for Tuesday positions), free. Cross-checks: OFR Hedge Fund Monitor (quarterly), Fed and dealer estimates (voices register, dated).
- **Rights:** narrow flag in positioning & flows and plumbing & rates. Level flags (ruled 6 Oct 2026): on the 10y series, ≥35% of OI elevated and ≥40% extreme (record 45%); on the six-contract aggregate, the 90th and 97th percentiles of its own full TFF history, set after the first pull rather than as fixed numbers. "Basis-unwind watch" is a coincidence flag only — a level flag on either series plus any two of: SR-35 at 2σ; MOVE one-month change at 2σ; SOFR–IORB widening or a sponsored-repo volume shock; 10y swap-spread two-week change beyond 2σ. Candidate input to the T&B Liquidity & Funding Stress overlay through the one-at-a-time queue — not wired in this batch. No directional rights; explicitly not a sentiment read, and never printed as "hedge funds are short bonds".
- **Gate:** calibration ledger — March 2020, April 2025, Aug–Oct 2025 (record and ease), the 2026 compression — before any panel or overlay wiring; Daily wiring after D4/D6. T&B overlay (ruled 6 Oct 2026): report-only through the first calibration cycle; a one-at-a-time queue entry for the Liquidity & Funding Stress overlay is created only when ST-B3-14's ledger is dated and shows the unwind-watch flag led or coincided with the overlay's own stress readings in 2020 and 2025.
