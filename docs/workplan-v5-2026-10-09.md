## What this schedule does not compress

- **Evidence.** The scorecard's base rates need ~20 sessions (mid-November) before the engine registers; its formation sample runs into 2027; the calibration ledgers (SR-30, SR-32, SR-36, net liquidity) need history that does not yet exist.
- **Category 2** (≈ 74 h) stays conditional — triggered, not scheduled.
- **Book B candidates** need weeks of drafts before packets mean anything; the attention budget counts approvals, not drafts.
- **Live orders**: Gate 3, behind months of paper dwell; not before 2027.

## When the system proposes and tracks trades (ruled 9 Oct)

Sun 25 Oct: the first Weekly with Book B drafts from the scanner (setup, level, invalidation, time stop, falsifiers, the gate's pre-check), graded from that day by the register's grader — taken, declined and ignored alike. ~26 Oct: the daily close names Book C setups whose conditions hold (the dealer block). 1 Nov: Book A's stance with scenario set #1. Mid-November: the first paper engine, ledger entries only. The operator is the only hand that approves, places or records.

## Rules carried forward

Paper trading only; sessions never place orders. No Brookfield-complex trades (blocklist in Python and SQLite). One task, one machine; every paste names its session. Sessions may validate, deploy and use git; they print unit commands, never run them; `decide.py record/set-status`, narrative confirm/reject and `.env` are the operator's. A push to main is a box write and happens only inside the windows (weekdays 09:05–15:40 and 17:20–23:30 ET; weekends open, clear of Sunday 05:00). CI is the validate; the laptop's full gate suite is optional. No secrets and no real position figures in any chat, commit or file. Code gates never read the live store. Reports never fetch.
